# Saffron & Sage Home Kitchen: Delivery and Pickup

## Delivery Areas and Charges

Saffron & Sage Home Kitchen delivers in these areas of Karachi. The delivery charge depends on the area:

- DHA and Clifton: delivery charge is Rs. 800.
- PECHS and Bahadurabad: delivery charge is Rs. 1,000.
- Gulshan-e-Iqbal and North Nazimabad: delivery charge is Rs. 300.

Delivery is not available outside these areas.

## Delivery Timing

Saffron & Sage Home Kitchen delivery slots are chosen when the order is confirmed. Chef Noor delivers the food hot, about one hour before the time the customer wants to serve it. Deliveries are made every day from 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

## Pickup

Customers can also collect their order from the Saffron & Sage Home Kitchen in Gulshan-e-Iqbal instead of using delivery. Pickup is free. The customer receives the exact pickup address on WhatsApp once the order is confirmed.

## Trays and Containers

Saffron & Sage Home Kitchen delivers food in sealed foil trays. Serving spoons are included. Chafing dishes and serving staff are not included in any package.
