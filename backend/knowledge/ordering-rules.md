# Saffron & Sage Home Kitchen: Ordering Rules

## How to Place an Order

Customers place orders by sending a WhatsApp message to Chef Noor on 0300-0000000. The order is only confirmed after Chef Noor replies and the advance payment is received. The chat assistant cannot confirm orders. It passes order requests to Chef Noor.

## Minimum Order

The minimum order is food for 10 people. Smaller orders are not accepted.

## Advance Notice

Orders of up to 50 guests need at least 3 days' notice. Orders for more than 50 guests need at least 7 days' notice.

## Advance Payment

An advance payment of 50 percent of the total is required to confirm an order. The remaining 50 percent is paid on delivery or pickup. Payment can be made by bank transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash, or in cash on delivery for the remaining amount.

## Order Hours

Chef Noor accepts new orders every day from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

## Custom Menus

Customers can ask for changes to a package or a custom menu. Custom menus are priced by Chef Noor personally, so the chat assistant cannot give a price for them.
