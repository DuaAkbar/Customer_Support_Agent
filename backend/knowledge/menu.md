# Saffron & Sage Home Kitchen: Menu and Prices

Saffron & Sage Home Kitchen is a home catering business in Karachi, run by Chef Noor. All meat is halal. Every dish is cooked fresh on the day of the event. Prices are in Pakistani Rupees (Rs.).

## Main Dishes

Chicken Biryani costs Rs. 6,500 per tray. One tray serves 10 people. Chicken Biryani is made with basmati rice, chicken, yogurt, fried onions and a house spice blend. It does not contain nuts.

Beef Nihari costs Rs. 8,000 per tray. One tray serves 10 people. Beef Nihari is slow-cooked beef thickened with wheat flour, so it contains gluten.

Chicken Karahi costs Rs. 9,000 per tray. One tray serves 10 people. Chicken Karahi is made with chicken, tomatoes, ginger and green chilies.

Mutton Qorma costs Rs. 12,000 per tray. One tray serves 10 people.

## Barbecue

Seekh Kabab costs Rs. 4,500 for 20 pieces.

Chicken Malai Boti costs Rs. 5,500 for 20 pieces.

## Vegetarian Dishes

Vegetable Pulao costs Rs. 5,000 per tray. One tray serves 10 people.

Daal Makhni costs Rs. 5,000 per tray. One tray serves 10 people.

## Sides

Naan costs Rs. 60 per piece. Raita and Salad together cost Rs. 1,500 per tray, and one tray serves 10 people.

## Desserts

Kheer costs Rs. 3,500 per tray. One tray serves 10 people.

Gulab Jamun costs Rs. 3,000 for 20 pieces.

## Packages

The Small Gathering package is for 10 to 15 guests. It includes Chicken Biryani, Chicken Karahi, Naan, Raita and Salad, and Kheer. It costs Rs. 1,400 per head.

The Family Dinner package is for 20 to 30 guests. It includes Chicken Biryani, Beef Nihari, Seekh Kabab, Naan, Raita and Salad, and Gulab Jamun. It costs Rs. 1,800 per head.

The Grand Feast package is for 40 to 60 guests. It includes Chicken Biryani, Mutton Qorma, Chicken Karahi, Seekh Kabab, Naan, Raita and Salad, Kheer, and Gulab Jamun. It costs Rs. 2,400 per head.
