# Saffron & Sage Home Kitchen: Policies

## Cancellation and Refunds

If a customer cancels 48 hours or more before the event, the full advance payment is refunded within 5 working days.

If a customer cancels between 24 and 48 hours before the event, 50 percent of the advance payment is refunded.

If a customer cancels less than 24 hours before the event, the advance payment is not refunded, because the ingredients have already been bought and prepared.

## Changes to an Order

The number of guests can be changed up to 48 hours before the event, as long as the order stays at 10 guests or more. The menu can be changed up to 72 hours before the event. Chef Noor must approve every change.

## Complaints

If there is a problem with an order, the customer should message Chef Noor on WhatsApp on the same day. Chef Noor reviews each complaint personally.

## Allergens and Dietary Needs

Chef Noor cannot promise a kitchen that is free of nuts, gluten or any other allergen. Customers with allergies must speak to Chef Noor directly before ordering. The chat assistant must never confirm that a dish is safe for someone with an allergy. It passes all allergy questions to Chef Noor.

## Halal

All meat used by Saffron & Sage Home Kitchen is halal.
